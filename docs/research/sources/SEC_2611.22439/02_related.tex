\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\noindent\textbf{Collaborative Perception and Ego-Side Demand Priors.} Multi-agent collaborative perception (CP) overcomes single-vehicle line-of-sight limits via intermediate bird's-eye-view (BEV) feature sharing, optimally balancing representation richness and bandwidth~\cite{opv2v,v2vnet,v2xvit,dairv2x,v2xseq,v2v4real}. To address severe occlusions, prior studies quantify spatial ambiguity via Bayesian deep learning~\cite{kendall2017uncertainties}, scene completion~\cite{monoscene}, or confidence maps~\cite{where2comm}. However, these uncertainty metrics are predominantly exploited retrospectively at the receiver for feature re-weighting~\cite{coalign,hm_vit}, failing to prevent the upstream transmission of ego-visible redundancy. In contrast, we formulate a proactive ego-side saliency map (demand) to gate transmission, explicitly focusing scarce bandwidth on ego-missed regions.

\noindent\textbf{Communication-Efficient Collaboration.} To satisfy strict bandwidth constraints, early CP methods use coarse link-level handshakes~\cite{who2com,when2com}. Fine-grained methods instead prune spatial content via sender-side top-$k$ selection~\cite{where2comm}, receiver-guided queries~\cite{hm_vit,quest2024}, channel filtering~\cite{whispernet}, robust aggregation~\cite{ermvp}, codebook-based filling~\cite{codefilling}, pragmatic communication~\cite{pragmatic}, or demand-supply fusion~\cite{cosdh}. Despite this progress, two conventions persist. Selection is usually scored by one party, either the sender's own confidence or a receiver-side query, so cells the ego already observes can still consume budget and the decision never becomes joint. Refinement, in turn, typically follows masking, so the transmitted support exceeds the selected cells and no longer matches the nominal budget. SEC reverses both, forming a single agreement between demand and supply before any token is committed and moving refinement ahead of masking, so the support is exactly $k$ cells by construction. Recent work explores temporal and spatial awareness~\cite{scope2023}, cooperative reconstruction~\cite{core2023}, and contrastive view learning~\cite{collabviews}. These methods also differ in scope. Collaborator selection chooses whom to communicate with~\cite{who2com,when2com,select2col}, whereas token selection chooses what to send. They are complementary, and SEC addresses the latter.

\noindent\textbf{Sparse Deformable Alignment and Discrete Optimization.} Aggregating multi-agent features requires efficiency and resilience to pose drift. Dense Transformers capture global dependencies~\cite{v2xvit,v2xvitv2,dosovitskiy2020image,swin,bevformer,bevformerv2,bevnext} but incur severe complexity, while rigid grids fail under localization and asynchronous noise~\cite{coalign,trafalign,bevflow}. We adapt an 8-point deformable sampler from deformable attention~\cite{deformable_detr} to sparse tokens, using projections without bias terms to absorb misalignments while rejecting padding artifacts. Finally, discrete top-$k$ truncation injects gradient oscillations; a short foreground warm-up stabilizes training without changing the inference architecture.
