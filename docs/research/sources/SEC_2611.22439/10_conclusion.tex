\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This paper has introduced SEC, a novel bandwidth-adaptive framework that replaces sender-driven pruning with bilateral demand-supply consensus. By systematically coupling a single-round multiplicative gate with an anti-dilation selection pipeline, SEC strictly enforces exact token budgets while precisely isolating critical ego-missed information. Comprehensive evaluations demonstrate that SEC substantially outperforms existing methods under stringent communication constraints.
