\section{Conclusion}

On the 900-scene Town10HD corpus, two agents missed the same object 1.80 to
2.52 times as often as independence predicts, depending on the detector
pairing, and a calibration fitted on 450 scenes predicted the joint miss rate
on held-out scenes and illuminations. A closing rule that trusted the latest
report left a missed object in 80.4\% of tasks.

The open step is the decision experiment. We will run the miss-risk rule with
the calibrated estimator, sweep its tolerance, and compare it with the naive
rule at equal verification rate, with paired bootstrap intervals on the
difference. Until that run exists, the decision-level claim stops at the cost
of unconditional trust.
