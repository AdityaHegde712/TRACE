\section{Conclusion}

On the 900-scene Town10HD corpus, two agents missed the same object 1.80 to
2.52 times as often as independence predicts, depending on the detector
pairing, and a calibration fitted on 450 scenes predicted the joint miss rate
on held-out scenes and illuminations. A closing rule that trusted the latest
report left a missed object in 80.4\% of tasks.

We swept the miss-risk tolerance with the calibrated estimator. It gave four
operating points between 97.4\% and 100\% verification, with false clearance
from 2.3\% to zero. At equal verification cost the rule closed no fewer unsafe
tasks than random choice among the tasks it could close. The decision-level
claim therefore stops at the cost of unconditional trust. A task-level
estimator is the open step.
