# 📋 Project Meeting Documentation Guide

### 🔍 Meetings to Track

#### 👨‍🏫 1. Advisor Meetings (Team + Project Advisor)
*   👥 **Participants:** All students in your project team and your project advisor.
*   📌 **Minimum Expectation:** At least **1 advisor meeting every two weeks**.

#### 👥 2. Team-Only Meetings (Students Only)
*   👥 **Participants:** Only the students in your project team.
*   📌 **Minimum Expectation:** At least **1 team-only meeting every week**.

---

### 💻 GitHub Requirements

1. **Folder Setup:** Create a `/meetings/` folder in your GitHub project.
2. **Naming Convention:** Use one note per meeting. Examples:
   *   📄 `YYYY-MM-DD-advisor.md`
   *   📄 `YYYY-MM-DD-team.md`
3. **Format Options:** Notes must be written in **Markdown (.md)** or **text (.txt)**.
4. **Content Requirement:** Include **action items** and **ownership** clearly.
5. **Review Schedule:** These records will be monitored **every two weeks**.
6. **Starter Resource:** 📁 [Meeting Notes Template.txt](#) 📄

---

### 💡 Core Principles

> 🎯 **Quality Over Quantity**
> Quality is not about length—it's about **specificity, traceability, and actionability**.

> 🤝 **Meaningful Engagement**
> Meetings with your advisor must be **meaningful**.

> 📅 **Submission Scope**
> This submission covers **all meetings** from the beginning of the semester leading up to the **Workbook 1 assignment**.
